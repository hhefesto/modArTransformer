For Tai-Danae Bradley — meaning is enriched-categorical

  • The softmax output is a [0,1]-enriched copresheaf π(·|ctx) — the representable hom hˣ = L(x,−), the meaning of the context.
  • The loss is the semantic distance: relative entropy to the Dirac ground-truth meaning (the Shannon t→1 limit of the magnitude / Tsallis-entropy invariant).
  • Type-checked in Agda: Semantics/{Enriched,Copresheaf,Language,Meaning}.agda.

Still rough: a toy modular-arithmetic language, not natural language; enrichment laws recorded but not proved for the learned model; the copresheaf drives the loss but the learned representations aren't yet shown to realize the enriched structure.

For Conal Elliott — differentiation is a functor

  • The forward pass is one morphism; the backward pass is its transpose in Dual AddFun (cartesian ↦ cocartesian) — gradients by the chain rule, no hand-written backward.
  • Type-checked in Agda: Cat/{D,Dual,Grad,VecPrim}.agda (≈ "The Simple Essence of AD").
  • Speed: the runtime is a Wengert tape — each node visited once, no diamond recomputation (~27× over the point-free form: 17.7 → 0.67 s/epoch).

Still rough: the fast path is an operational tape, not a pure categorical interpretation; Compile-to-Categories (toCcc) didn't pan out for attention's reverse mode; the loss uses a shift-invariant detached-max for stability; graph-parallelism unrealized.
